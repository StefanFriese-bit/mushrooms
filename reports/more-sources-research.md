# More trusted websites for the 40 species without a page — for your approval (07/10/2026)

**Stefan's ruling (07/10/2026): NatureSpot only** (not Rogers Mushrooms). Result: 12 new pages — see
`reports/batch-3-naturespot.md`.

The 40 species on the approved list without a page (see `reports/batch-2-cross-check.md`, end) need a second trusted
website. **Nothing below has been used yet.** Your approval comes first; the two-websites-per-fact rule stays as it is.

What I looked for: a UK website, run by a recognised organisation or author, that describes fungi species by species in
its own article (not readers' comments or recorders' photo captions), and that a script may read.

## Recommended: NatureSpot (naturespot.org)

- **Who:** a registered charity for the wildlife of Leicestershire and Rutland, run by volunteers, overseen by a board of
  local naturalists and conservation professionals, working with the county recorders and the county records centre;
  supported by the Leicestershire and Rutland Wildlife Trust and the local councils (its own About page).
- **What a species page gives:** a description in its own words (cap, gills, stem, ring, texture, colour), similar
  species, habitat, when to see it, UK status. Rarely: a spore colour (5 of the 26 pages below, e.g. Orange Mosscap
  "spore print white") or a size (2). **Never edibility** on these 26.
- **Covers 26 of the 40:** Butter Waxcap, Yellow Club, Common Jellyspot, Green Elfcup, Persistent Waxcap, Spangle
  Waxcap, Orange Mosscap, Wrinkled Fieldcap, Wrinkled Club, Beech Jellydisc, Glutinous Waxcap, Flame Shield, Holly
  Parachute, Dewdrop Mottlegill, Conical Brittlestem (filed as *Parasola conopilus*), Powderpuff Bracket (as *Postia
  ptychogaster*), Beechmast Candlesnuff, Steely Bonnet, Bolete Mould, Thimble Morel, Dark Honey Fungus, Pipe Club,
  Bleeding Broadleaf Crust, Matt Bolete, Conifer Blueing Bracket (as *Postia caesia*), Dung-loving Deconica.
- **What it could unlock:** of the 29 species with one trusted site or none, **19 would have two** (the first trusted
  site plus NatureSpot). Most are not-edible species, where your rule of 06/10 lets the "not edible" verdict rest on one
  site and the descriptive facts need two — exactly what NatureSpot gives. Of the 11 held species it may unlock the
  **Wrinkled Fieldcap** (held because gills, ring and habitat were on one site only; NatureSpot describes all three).
  It does **not** fix the held edible species — their missing facts (spore print, cap size, edibility) are the ones
  NatureSpot does not give. Whether each page can really be written is checked fact by fact, only after your OK.
- **Caution I would apply:** only the species account counts. The recorders' notes under the photos ("cap 3.5 cm
  diameter") are excluded, the same way page comments are excluded elsewhere.

## Possible, with a warning: Rogers Mushrooms (rogersmushrooms.com)

- **Who:** Roger Phillips' mushroom site (his text, © Rogers Plants Ltd) — the author of the standard British field
  guide *Mushrooms*. Its pages give cap size, spore print, edibility, habitat and season — the facts NatureSpot lacks.
- **Warnings:** (1) its home page now carries links to gambling sites, so the site is no longer kept the way a
  reference site should be; (2) its A–Z index is broken (the "R" list is empty), so coverage cannot be measured
  properly — 9 of the 40 found through it: Common Jellyspot, Green Elfcup, *Ganoderma resinaceum*, Meadow Puffball (as
  *Vascellum pratense*), Beechmast Candlesnuff, Heath Navel (as *Omphalina ericetorum*), Bleeding Broadleaf Crust,
  Conifer Blueing Bracket (as *Oligoporus caesius*), Dung-loving Deconica; (3) its fact box and its own text can disagree
  (Field Mushroom: box "purplish to black", text "spore print brown") — only the text would count; (4) its names are
  old (the book's), so each page must be matched with care.
- It would add a second site for 2 more of the 29 (*Ganoderma resinaceum*, Heath Navel), and may give the Meadow
  Puffball's spore colour (held).
- **My suggestion:** only if you are comfortable with the warnings; otherwise leave it out.

## Looked at and not suitable

- **The Wildlife Trusts** (wildlifetrusts.org): about 30 popular species only; of the 40, just the Green Elfcup.
- **Fungi of Great Britain and Ireland** (fungi.myspecies.info): refuses automated reading (HTTP 403 to every request
  from here). I would not try to get round that.
- **Scottish Fungi** (sites.google.com/site/scottishfungi): news and "fungus of the month" articles, not species
  pages.
- Already searched for batch 2: Wikipedia, First Nature (every older name), the Woodland Trust, Kew, the Natural History
  Museum, Plantlife.
